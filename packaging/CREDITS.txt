Gorilla Drive - be a Gorilla Tag gorilla in The Long Drive

Needs your own copies of The Long Drive and Gorilla Tag. Nothing from either game is
included: the gorilla's body, hands and fur are read from your Gorilla Tag folder each
time the game starts.
